## What I'm working on

My main interests are in ML applications to health and life sciences, working on new methods in optimization, Bayesian experimental design and causal inference on clinical data. 
 
---

## Selected open source

**[BExTE](https://github.com/TristanFauvel/BExTE)** — Bayesian Extrapolation of Treatment Effects: a clinical trial-design simulation tool that operationalizes my EMA-funded research on extrapolating treatment effects across populations. [Docs](https://tristanfauvel.github.io/BExTE/).

**[statrl](https://github.com/StatisticalRL/statrl)** — contributor to the Statistical Reinforcement Learning Toolkit, an open-source library for statistical RL.

## Other projects

I've developed the AI platform [SEARIDE](https://searide.baysic-labs.com/) for seahorse reidentification from photographs for citizen science programs.
